Methodology

how a risk score is produced

SlopeGuard combines terrain, environmental and historical indicators into a single normalized risk score. The pipeline below is fully implemented in this prototype using synthetic and demo data; each stage is architected so a production data source can be substituted in without changing downstream code.

DATA COLLECTION
Rainfall, soil, terrain, satellite, historical records
↓
DATA CLEANING
Outlier removal, gap-filling, unit normalization
↓
FEATURE ENGINEERING
Cumulative rainfall, slope/aspect, drainage proximity
↓
GIS PROCESSING
Grid/point alignment, coordinate reference systems
↓
XGBOOST MODEL
Gradient-boosted classifier / regressor
↓
RISK SCORE (0–100)
LOW · MODERATE · HIGH · VERY HIGH
↓
MAP VISUALIZATION
GeoJSON risk zones on the interactive map
↓
EARLY WARNING
Threshold-based alerts to authorities and users
